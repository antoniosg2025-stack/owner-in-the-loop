# Owner in the loop

**Learning a business owner's reward from ratings and corrections when the owner is only sometimes watching.**

Interactive proposal: **https://hitl.antonio-sainz.com** · Full research portfolio: https://research.antonio-sainz.com

Prepared for the Intelligent Robot Learning Lab (University of Alberta). Two experiments build on the lab's recent work:

1. **Ratings vs. preferences** (R4, Kharyal, Muslimani & Taylor, ICML 2026), scored with Kendall tau-b alignment (TAC, Muslimani et al., RLJ 2025).
2. **The owner as a monitor** (Monitored MDPs, Kazemipour, Parisi, Taylor & Bowling, ICML 2025).

Run: `python tools/experiments.py` (numpy, scipy). The owner is simulated from the decision profile of the main portfolio (case 01); comparisons, not absolute numbers, are the result.

Author: Juan Antonio Sainz Gálvez.
